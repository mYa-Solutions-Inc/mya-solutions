Impact · Public benefit reporting

Ten percent of all earnings. Written into the bylaws.

An M-class share obliges mYa Solutions to direct 10% of all earnings to charitable causes. LeadLifeLaugh Inc., a California benefit corporation, exists to hold the company to it — and California requires a benefit corporation to report on its public benefit every year. Those reports are posted here.

Who holds the obligation

A separate corporation, built to protect the mission.

LeadLifeLaugh Inc. was formed as a mission-holding entity: its job is to hold governance rights in mYa Solutions for the purpose of protecting its charitable and public benefit commitments over time, and to direct the categories and recipients of the giving that arises from them.
